Hey there! Sunflowers are amazing! Did you know they follow the sun? It's true—sunflowers turn toward the sunlight during the day. This is called heliotropism, and it helps them grow tall and strong. Want to plant your own? Choose a sunny spot, give them lots of water, and watch them bloom in no time. Sunflower seeds are yummy and nutritious, while their oil can be used for cooking or skincare. These bright flowers not only brighten up any garden but also help bees with pollen. So, next time you see one, remember: sunflowers make the world a brighter place!
